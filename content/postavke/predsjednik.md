  **Poštovani prijatelji, članovi i posjetioci,**


  Dobro došli na internet stranicu Rotary kluba Banja Luka.


  Predsjedavanje Klubom u rotarijanskoj 2026/27. godini za mene predstavlja veliku čast, ali prije svega odgovornost da sačuvamo ono što su generacije naših članova gradile i da istovremeno otvorimo prostor za nove ideje, ljude i projekte.


  Rotary klub Banja Luka okuplja ljude različitih profesija, iskustava i životnih puteva, povezane uvjerenjem da znanje, vrijeme i lični angažman imaju najveću vrijednost kada ih stavimo u službu drugih. Naša snaga nije samo u pojedinačnim humanitarnim akcijama, već u povjerenju koje gradimo, partnerstvima koja stvaramo i spremnosti da prepoznamo potrebe zajednice i na njih odgovorimo konkretnim djelovanjem.


  U godini pred nama nastavićemo da razvijamo projekte po kojima je naš Klub već prepoznatljiv, uključujući tradicionalni humanitarni bal i dodjelu Povelje „Spiridon Špiro Bocarić“. Istovremeno želimo pokrenuti nove aktivnosti usmjerene na obrazovanje i razvoj mladih, podršku zdravstvenim i socijalnim ustanovama te pomoć pojedincima i porodicama kojima je solidarnost zajednice najpotrebnija.


  Posebnu pažnju posvetićemo uključivanju mladih, saradnji sa obrazovnim ustanovama i stvaranju prilika da učenici i studenti razvijaju znanje, samopouzdanje, odgovornost i osjećaj pripadnosti zajednici. Vjerujemo da je ulaganje u mlade jedan od najtrajnijih načina da služimo Banjoj Luci i njenoj budućnosti.


  Jedan od naših prioriteta biće i dodatno otvaranje Kluba prema građanima, institucijama, privredi, drugim rotari klubovima i organizacijama sa kojima dijelimo iste vrijednosti. Najbolji rezultati nastaju kada udružimo različita iskustva i mogućnosti oko jasno postavljenog cilja.


  Nastavićemo da jačamo i sam Klub – kroz međusobno povjerenje, aktivno učešće članova, prijem novih ljudi i njegovanje prijateljstva koje je oduvijek bilo temelj Rotarija. Želimo da budemo klub koji poštuje svoju tradiciju, ali se ne plaši promjena; klub u kojem se dobre ideje pretvaraju u održive projekte, a članstvo podrazumijeva i pripadnost i djelovanje.


  Na ovoj stranici možete pratiti naše projekte, aktivnosti i događaje, upoznati se sa radom Kluba i saznati na koji način možete da nam se pridružite ili podržite neku od naših inicijativa. Biće nam drago da čujemo vaše prijedloge i da zajedno razmotrimo svaku ideju koja može doprinijeti našoj zajednici.

  Dear friends, members and visitors,


  Welcome to the website of the Rotary Club Banja Luka.


  Serving as President of the Club in the 2026–27 Rotary year is a great honor for me, but above all a responsibility: to preserve what generations of our members have built, while at the same time opening space for new ideas, people and projects.


  The Rotary Club Banja Luka brings together people of different professions, experiences and walks of life, united by the belief that knowledge, time and personal commitment are of the greatest value when we put them in the service of others. Our strength lies not only in individual humanitarian initiatives, but in the trust we build, the partnerships we create, and our readiness to recognize the needs of our community and respond to them with concrete action.


  In the year ahead, we will continue to develop the projects for which our Club is already known, including the traditional charity ball and the presentation of the “Spiridon Špiro Bocarić” Charter. At the same time, we wish to launch new activities focused on the education and development of young people, support for health and social care institutions, and assistance to individuals and families who most need the solidarity of our community.


  We will pay special attention to engaging young people, cooperating with educational institutions, and creating opportunities for pupils and students to develop knowledge, self-confidence, responsibility and a sense of belonging to the community. We believe that investing in young people is one of the most lasting ways to serve Banja Luka and its future.


  One of our priorities will also be to open the Club further to citizens, institutions, the business community, other Rotary clubs and organizations with which we share the same values. The best results come when we bring together different experiences and capabilities around a clearly defined goal.


  We will continue to strengthen the Club itself – through mutual trust, the active participation of our members, welcoming new people, and nurturing the fellowship that has always been the foundation of Rotary. We want to be a club that respects its tradition but is not afraid of change; a club in which good ideas grow into sustainable projects, and where membership means both belonging and action.


  On this website you can follow our projects, activities and events, learn about the work of the Club, and find out how you can join us or support one of our initiatives. We will be glad to hear your suggestions and to consider together every idea that can contribute to our community.


  I invite you to follow our work, talk with us, and be part of the change we want to bring about. Because the value of Rotary is best seen when shared effort grows into real and lasting benefit for others.


  *Yours in Rotary,*
